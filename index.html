01 — A LITTLE REMINDER

You don't have to have it all figured out.

Mamma, I know life hasn't always been easy. There are memories, experiences and difficult chapters that sometimes take longer to understand.

But please remember that you don't have to be perfectly healed, perfectly strong or perfectly happy every single day.

You deserve kindness, patience and peace too.

02 — TWO DIFFERENT JOURNEYS

We both have a past.

I won't pretend that I don't have memories or experiences of my own.

We've both lived different chapters. We've both been shaped by things we cannot change.

But our past doesn't have to write every page of our future.

03 — WHAT COULD BE

What if we simply tried to understand each other?

Not two perfect people. Not two people without scars.

Just two people willing to be honest, patient and considerate of one another.

Learning to communicate. Making time. Building trust. And choosing each other freely.

04 — FROM MY HEART

One thing I want you to know.

I cannot promise that life will always be easy. But I can promise to approach what we build with honesty, patience and genuine intentions.

I don't want you to feel pressured to have all the answers today.

Whatever tomorrow holds, I hope we never forget that both our hearts deserve to be heard.
