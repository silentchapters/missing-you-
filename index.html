01 — A LITTLE REMINDER

You don't have to have it all figured out.

Mamma, I know life hasn't always been easy. There are memories, experiences and difficult chapters that sometimes take longer to understand.

But please remember that you don't have to be perfectly healed, perfectly strong or perfectly happy every single day.

You deserve kindness, patience and peace too.

02 — TWO DIFFERENT JOURNEYS

We both have a past.

I won't pretend that I don't have memories or experiences of my own.

We've both lived different chapters. We've both been shaped by things we cannot change.

But our past doesn't have to write every page of OUR FUTURE.

04 — THE THINGS THAT MATTER

Some things are worth building gently.

Not everything needs an immediate answer. Some things simply need time, understanding and two willing hearts.

Trust isn't built through beautiful promises alone. It grows through honesty, consistency and the little things we do when nobody is watching.

Healing has no universal timetable. We don't have to rush each other. Sometimes being patient is one of the kindest things we can offer.

We may not always see things the same way. But we can listen, communicate and try to understand what the other heart is saying.

A future cannot be built by one person alone. It takes two people who freely wants to be there, making room for one another through genuine effort.

Little things. Honest intentions. Mutual effort. That's where something meaningful can begin.
